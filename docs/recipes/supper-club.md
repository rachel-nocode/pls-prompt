EXACT RECIPES
Lemon pasta, base servings 2, 20 minutes: Pasta 200 g; Lemon 1 whole; Olive oil 30 ml; Parmesan 50 g; Basil 10 g. Steps: Cook pasta until tender and reserve a little water. Toss warm pasta with olive oil and lemon juice. Add Parmesan, loosen with pasta water, finish with basil.
Bean tacos, base servings 2, 15 minutes: Tortillas 6 whole; Cooked beans 300 g; Tomato 2 whole; Lime 1 whole; Yogurt 60 g. Steps: Warm beans and tortillas. Chop tomato and dress with lime juice. Fill tortillas with beans, tomato and yogurt.
Sunday pancakes, base servings 4, 25 minutes: Flour 240 g; Milk 300 ml; Egg 2 whole; Baking powder 8 g; Butter 30 g. Steps: Whisk flour and baking powder. Mix in milk and eggs. Cook spoonfuls in a buttered skillet until set, turning once. Use the same ingredient order everywhere.

BEHAVIOR
Start Lemon pasta at 2 people. Native Minus and Plus buttons and a visibly labeled integer People number input control serving count from 1 through 12. Give minus and plus accessible names 'Fewer people' and 'More people'. Plus/minus step the committed value and disable at limits. The number input commits on change; empty, fractional, nonfinite or out-of-range entries show a polite visible error and aria-invalid, retain previous quantities and checklist, and leave text editable; valid input clears error. On recipe selection retain the last valid people count, normalize input, clear all checks/errors and render its dish/ingredients/steps. Same recipe selection may reset checks. A committed serving change clears ingredient checks because quantities changed. No-op commit at the same value preserves checks. Quantities are base amount times people divided by base servings; show up to two decimal places without trailing zeros; whole items may have fractions, labeled singular/plural sensibly. Never round whole items up silently. Show 'Shopping list', checked count out of five, and each scaled amount beside a labeled native checkbox. Checking changes only that row's state; checked rows have strikethrough plus checkmark. Include Clear checks and Reset menu; reset selects Lemon pasta at 2 people and clears checks/errors.
